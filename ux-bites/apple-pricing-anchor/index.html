UX Bite Apple

Apple anchors you to the middle storage tier

Apple's storage and device pages lead with a mid-priced option, making the step up feel small and the entry tier feel limiting.

Look at how Apple lays out storage tiers: the smallest option is just big enough to feel tight, and the middle tier is positioned as the sensible default. Once your eye anchors there, the jump to the next tier reads as “only a little more” rather than “another expense.”

Steal this: the first price a user sees sets the reference point for every other price. Choose your anchor deliberately.
