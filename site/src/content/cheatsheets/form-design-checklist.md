Forms are where good products quietly lose users. Run through this before you ship.

## Structure

1. **Cut every optional field.** If you don't act on the data, don't ask for it.
2. **One column, top-aligned labels.** Predictable eye path, faster completion.
3. **Group related fields** with clear section headings.

## Input behaviour

4. **Use the right input type** (`email`, `tel`, `number`) so mobile keyboards adapt.
5. **Autofill-friendly names** (`autocomplete` attributes) — let the browser do the typing.
6. **Format as they type** for cards, phone numbers and dates.

## Errors & validation

7. **Validate on blur, not on submit.** Catch mistakes while the field is fresh.
8. **Errors next to the field**, in plain language, saying how to fix it.
9. **Never clear the form** on a failed submit.

## Trust & finishing

10. **Explain why** you need sensitive data, inline.
11. **Show progress** for multi-step flows.
12. **One primary button.** Make the next action unmistakable.

> Rule of thumb: every field is a small tax on completion. Spend them deliberately.
