Pricing pages are where intent turns into revenue — or evaporates. Run through this before
you publish.

## Clarity

1. **Lead with the plan most people should pick**, and mark it clearly.
2. **One value statement per tier**, not a wall of identical-looking features.
3. **Show the price, not "contact us"** wherever you possibly can.

## Anchoring & comparison

4. **Order tiers so the recommended one is the reference point.**
5. **Make differences scannable** — a feature table beats prose.
6. **Annual vs monthly toggle** with the saving shown explicitly.

## Trust & objections

7. **Name the get-out** ("cancel anytime", "no card required") near the CTA.
8. **Answer the top 3 objections** in a short FAQ below the tiers.
9. **Social proof close to the price** — logos or a quote, not buried at the bottom.

## The ask

10. **One unmistakable primary CTA per tier.** Don't make people hunt for "Buy".

> Rule of thumb: a visitor should understand which plan is for them, and why, in under ten
> seconds — without scrolling back up.
