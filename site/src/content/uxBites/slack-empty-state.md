A brand-new channel has no messages — the classic "empty state" problem. Slack uses that
moment to teach: it suggests inviting people, setting a topic, and posting a first
message. The emptiness becomes a checklist, not a dead end.

**Steal this:** treat every empty state as your best onboarding surface. The user is
looking right at it, and they have nothing else to do.
