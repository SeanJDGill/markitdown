Look at how Apple lays out storage tiers: the smallest option is just big enough to feel
tight, and the middle tier is positioned as the sensible default. Once your eye anchors
there, the jump to the next tier reads as "only a little more" rather than "another
expense."

**Steal this:** the first price a user sees sets the reference point for every other
price. Choose your anchor deliberately.
