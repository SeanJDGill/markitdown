---
term: "Loss aversion"
aliases: []
definition: "The tendency to prefer avoiding losses over acquiring equivalent gains — losing $10 hurts more than gaining $10 pleases. In UX it explains why streaks, expiring rewards and 'you'll lose your progress' framings are so motivating."
relatedTopics: ["retention", "notifications"]
---

Loss aversion is a cornerstone of behavioural economics and a powerful (and easy to
misuse) retention lever.
