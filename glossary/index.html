Glossary

UX terms, plainly

The vocabulary behind the teardowns, defined.

Inline validation (live validation)
Checking a form field for errors as the user fills it in — typically on blur — and showing feedback right next to the field, rather than waiting until the whole form is submitted.
Forms Checkout
Loss aversion
The tendency to prefer avoiding losses over acquiring equivalent gains — losing $10 hurts more than gaining $10 pleases. In UX it explains why streaks, expiring rewards and 'you'll lose your progress' framings are so motivating.
Retention Notifications
Progressive disclosure (staged disclosure)
Revealing information and options gradually, showing only what's relevant at each step instead of everything at once. It keeps interfaces simple for new users while still exposing depth on demand.
