---
term: "Progressive disclosure"
aliases: ["staged disclosure"]
definition: "Revealing information and options gradually, showing only what's relevant at each step instead of everything at once. It keeps interfaces simple for new users while still exposing depth on demand."
relatedTopics: ["onboarding", "forms"]
---

Progressive disclosure reduces cognitive load by deferring complexity until the user
actually needs it — the opposite of a wall of fields or settings.
