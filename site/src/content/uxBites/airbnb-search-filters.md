When you open Airbnb's filters and drag the price slider, the button doesn't just say
"Apply" — it says how many places match *right now*. You get instant feedback before
committing, so you never apply a combination that wipes out every result.

**Steal this:** preview the consequence of a filter before the user commits to it. A live
count turns guessing into steering.
