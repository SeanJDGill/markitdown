---
term: "Inline validation"
aliases: ["live validation"]
definition: "Checking a form field for errors as the user fills it in — typically on blur — and showing feedback right next to the field, rather than waiting until the whole form is submitted."
relatedTopics: ["forms", "checkout"]
---

Done well, inline validation feels like help. Done badly (validating on every keystroke
before the user finishes) it feels like nagging.
