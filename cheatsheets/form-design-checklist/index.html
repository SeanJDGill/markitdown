Cheatsheet

12 checks before you ship a form

A pre-flight checklist for any form — sign-up, checkout, settings or contact.

Forms are where good products quietly lose users. Run through this before you ship.

Structure

  1. Cut every optional field. If you don’t act on the data, don’t ask for it.
  2. One column, top-aligned labels. Predictable eye path, faster completion.
  3. Group related fields with clear section headings.

Input behaviour

  1. Use the right input type (email, tel, number) so mobile keyboards adapt.
  2. Autofill-friendly names (autocomplete attributes) — let the browser do the typing.
  3. Format as they type for cards, phone numbers and dates.

Errors & validation

  1. Validate on blur, not on submit. Catch mistakes while the field is fresh.
  2. Errors next to the field, in plain language, saying how to fix it.
  3. Never clear the form on a failed submit.

Trust & finishing

  1. Explain why you need sensitive data, inline.
  2. Show progress for multi-step flows.
  3. One primary button. Make the next action unmistakable.

Rule of thumb: every field is a small tax on completion. Spend them deliberately.
