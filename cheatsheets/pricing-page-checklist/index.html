Cheatsheet

10 checks for a pricing page that converts

A pre-flight checklist for SaaS and subscription pricing pages — clarity, anchoring and removing the last objection.

Pricing pages are where intent turns into revenue — or evaporates. Run through this before you publish.

Clarity

  1. Lead with the plan most people should pick, and mark it clearly.
  2. One value statement per tier, not a wall of identical-looking features.
  3. Show the price, not “contact us” wherever you possibly can.

Anchoring & comparison

  1. Order tiers so the recommended one is the reference point.
  2. Make differences scannable — a feature table beats prose.
  3. Annual vs monthly toggle with the saving shown explicitly.

Trust & objections

  1. Name the get-out (“cancel anytime”, “no card required”) near the CTA.
  2. Answer the top 3 objections in a short FAQ below the tiers.
  3. Social proof close to the price — logos or a quote, not buried at the bottom.

The ask

  1. One unmistakable primary CTA per tier. Don’t make people hunt for “Buy”.

Rule of thumb: a visitor should understand which plan is for them, and why, in under ten seconds — without scrolling back up.
