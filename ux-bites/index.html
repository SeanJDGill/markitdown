UX Bites

One idea at a time

Bite-sized teardowns — a single, specific UX detail worth noticing.

UX Bite Linear

Linear puts every action one keystroke away

Linear's command menu (Cmd+K) exposes the entire app to the keyboard, so power users never reach for the mouse.

UX Bite Apple

Apple anchors you to the middle storage tier

Apple's storage and device pages lead with a mid-priced option, making the step up feel small and the entry tier feel limiting.

UX Bite Google

Google shows results forming as you type

Autocomplete predicts your query before you finish, turning search into a conversation rather than a submit-and-wait.

UX Bite Airbnb

Airbnb shows the result count as you filter

Airbnb updates a live count of matching stays while you adjust filters, so you never apply a filter that returns nothing.

UX Bite Stripe

Stripe validates the moment you finish, not on submit

Stripe's card fields check themselves as you leave each one — errors surface early, never all at once at the end.

UX Bite Slack

Slack turns an empty channel into a prompt

Instead of a blank void, a new Slack channel shows you exactly what to do next.
