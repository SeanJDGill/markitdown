Handing over card details is the most anxious moment in any purchase. Stripe has spent
years removing every micro-friction and micro-doubt from that moment — and most of its
craft is invisible until you look for it.

## Format as the user types

Card numbers gain spaces in the right places, the expiry adds its own slash, and the card
brand icon appears as soon as it's known. The user never wonders whether they're doing it
right — the field confirms it in real time.

<Insight number={1} title="Confirm correctness continuously">
  Live formatting is constant, quiet reassurance. Every space and icon says "yes, this is
  working" without a single error message.
</Insight>

## Validate kindly, at the right time

Stripe checks each field as you leave it — never mid-keystroke, never all at once at
submit. Errors appear next to the field, in plain language, the instant they're knowable.

<PullQuote cite="Stripe">
  Good validation feels like a helpful colleague looking over your shoulder, not a bouncer
  at the door.
</PullQuote>

## Engineer for everyone

The fields are real inputs with proper labels, full keyboard support and announced errors —
so the same checkout works with a screen reader or a password manager. Accessibility here
isn't a compliance afterthought; it's what makes the flow robust for everyone.

<Insight number={2} title="Accessible is just robust">
  Labelled fields, keyboard support and announced errors help screen-reader users — and
  also autofill, password managers and stressed thumbs on a train.
</Insight>

## The takeaway

Stripe defuses payment anxiety through accumulated detail: live formatting, kind
validation and genuinely accessible fields turn the scariest step into the smoothest one.
