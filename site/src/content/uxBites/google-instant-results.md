You rarely finish typing a Google query. Suggestions appear after a few characters, often
guessing exactly what you meant — and frequently saving you from a typo or a dead-end
phrasing. The search box feels less like a form and more like a back-and-forth.

**Steal this:** predict and suggest while the user types. Every character you save them is
friction removed, and every suggestion is a chance to steer them toward a query that works.
