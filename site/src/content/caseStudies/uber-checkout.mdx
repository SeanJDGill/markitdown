The old taxi experience ended with the worst part: fumbling for cash, watching the meter,
working out a tip. Uber's defining insight was that the best checkout is one the user never
sees at all.

## Move payment out of the moment

By collecting a payment method once, up front, Uber removes money from the end of the
journey. You arrive, you leave. The transaction happens silently in the background.

<Insight number={1} title="Collect once, charge invisibly">
  Any payment you can capture ahead of time is a payment that doesn't interrupt the moment
  of value. Front-load the friction so the climax is frictionless.
</Insight>

## Make the price knowable, not a surprise

Upfront fares replaced the anxiety of a ticking meter. Knowing the cost before you commit
removes the background worry that used to shadow every taxi ride.

<PullQuote cite="Uber">
  Remove the meter and you remove the anxiety. Certainty is a feature.
</PullQuote>

## Reduce the whole thing to one tap

Requesting a ride collapses destination, payment and confirmation into a single primary
action. Everything the user pre-configured means the live decision is just "yes, go".

<Screenshot
  src="/placeholders/screen-b.svg"

  caption="Pre-set defaults turn a multi-step transaction into one tap."
  number={2}

<Insight number={2} title="The best step is no step">
  Don't optimise a friction-filled step — see whether you can delete it. The fastest
  checkout is the one that isn't there.
</Insight>

## The takeaway

Uber didn't make paying faster; it made paying vanish. Capture payment once, guarantee the
price, and reduce the live decision to a single tap.
