About

Why we tear things down

Teardown is an independent publication that studies how the best digital products are actually designed — screen by screen, decision by decision. Inspired by the format pioneered by Built for Mars, every teardown is hands-on: we sign up, click through, and document the choices most people never notice.

How we work

We publish four kinds of content:

  • Case studies — deep, long-form teardowns of a single product flow.
  • UX bites — a single, specific detail worth stealing.
  • Cheatsheets — practical decision aids and checklists.
  • Glossary — plain-English definitions of the terms we use.

Our method

We focus on the why, not just the what. A good teardown explains the psychology and trade-offs behind a design — so you can apply the principle, not just copy the pixels.
